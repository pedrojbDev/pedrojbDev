## 👨💻 A little about me

👋 Hi! I'm **Pedro Jesuino Batista**, a **Computer Engineering student and Software Development Intern at SENAI CIMATEC**, where I work on software engineering and applied AI projects in partnership with **HP Inc. (USA)**.

I love turning ideas into working systems — from **backend services and asynchronous workflows** to **AI/LLM integrations, APIs and model deployments**. My hands-on stack is **Python, FastAPI, Docker, Linux and GPU inference**, and I'm always chasing performance.

- 🔭 Currently building: backend services & applied AI systems
- 🎯 Interested in: **software engineering, AI systems and high-performance computing**
- 📍 Based in **Salvador, Bahia — Brazil** 🇧🇷
- 💬 Always up for a chat about tech and the future of development!
